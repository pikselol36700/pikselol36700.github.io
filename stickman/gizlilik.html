1. Bizim topladığımız bilgiler

PikselOL Games, Oyun aracılığıyla adınız, e-posta adresiniz, telefon numaranız veya konumunuz gibi kişisel bilgilerinizi toplamaz ve sunucularına göndermez. Oyunda zorunlu hesap açma veya giriş yoktur; isteğe bağlı Google Play ile giriş yalnızca bulut yedeği için kullanılır (2. bölüm).

Oyun ilerlemeniz (elmaslar, mağaza eşyaları, birim geliştirmeleri, görevler, zombi rekorları, galibiyet sayınız, kayıtlı maç) ve ayarlarınız (ses, titreşim, dil) cihazınızda saklanır. Oyunun çevrimiçi özellikleri Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer/Relay, Leaderboards, Cloud Save, Analytics) ile çalışır. Bu özelliklerin hepsi aynı anonim oyuncu kimliğini kullanır; ad, soyad veya e-posta istenmez. Klasik ve Zombi modlarındaki rakibi cihazınız yönetir.

Oyunda sohbet yoktur. Bağlantı için IP adresiniz Unity sunucuları tarafından işlenir; oda kodu yalnızca maç süresince geçerlidir.
  • Dünya sıralaması (Unity Leaderboards): Klasik modda bir maç kazandığınızda toplam klasik galibiyet sayınız, anonim oyuncu kimliğinize ve otomatik üretilmiş bir oyuncu adına bağlı olarak sıralamaya gönderilir. Bu ad ve galibiyet sayınız sıralamada diğer oyunculara görünür. Sıralamayı ana menüdeki Sıralama düğmesiyle açtığınızda ilk 10 oyuncu ve sizin sıranız Unity'den okunur.
  • Bulut yedeği (Unity Cloud Save): Google Play ile giriş yaptıysanız ya da Ayarlar → Yedek ve Hesap bölümünde kurtarma kodu aldıysanız oyun ilerlemeniz (elmaslar, mağaza eşyaları, birim geliştirmeleri, görev ilerlemesi ve alınan ödüller, biten maç ve galibiyet sayıları, zombi rekorları) anonim oyuncu kimliğinize bağlı olarak Unity sunucularına kaydedilir; her maçtan sonra ve oyun arka plana geçerken kendiliğinden güncellenir. Ayarlarınız ve yarım kalan maç yedeklenmez. Kurtarma kodu, anonim hesabınıza kullanıcı adı ve şifre olarak bağlanır: kodu bilen kişi yedeğe erişebilir; kodu kimseyle paylaşmayın. Yeni telefonda kodu girdiğinizde yedek o cihaza yüklenir.
  • Google Play ile giriş (Google Play Games Hizmetleri, Unity Authentication): Yedeğinizin yeni telefonda geri gelmesi için Oyun, Google Play Games hesabınızla giriş yapmayı önerir. Giriş yapıldığında Google, Oyun'a tek kullanımlık bir yetki kodu verir; Unity bu kodla Google Play Games oyuncu kimliğinizi doğrular ve bulut yedeğinizi bu kimliğe bağlar. Aynı Google hesabıyla başka bir telefonda yedek bulunursa yüklenip yüklenmeyeceği size sorulur. E-posta adresiniz ve Google şifreniz Oyun'a verilmez. Google Play Games, Google'ın gizlilik politikasına tabidir; Play Games profilinizi ve ayarlarınızı Play Games uygulamasından yönetebilirsiniz.
  • Kullanım istatistiği (Unity Analytics): Oyunu geliştirmek için bir maç başladığında ve bittiğinde oyun modu, zorluk, maçın kazanılıp kazanılmadığı, ulaşılan çağ ve maç süresi; bunlarla birlikte cihaz bilgisi (model, işletim sistemi, dil, ülke) anonim oyuncu kimliğinize bağlı olarak Unity'ye gönderilir. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de kullanım istatistiği yalnızca onay penceresinde izin verirseniz toplanır.
  • Bildirimler: İzin verirseniz (izin ilk maçınızı bitirdikten sonra bir kez sorulur) Oyun "görev ödülün hazır", "ordun seni bekliyor" gibi hatırlatmaları cihazınızın kendisinde kurar; bunun için hiçbir veri gönderilmez. İzni Android ayarlarından kapatabilirsiniz.

Stickman Ages – Privacy Policy

Developer: PikselOL Games · Last updated: November 16, 2026 (Google Play sign-in, cloud backup, world ranking, usage statistics and in-game account deletion added)

This privacy policy explains what information is collected and how it is used when you play Stickman Ages ("the Game"), a mobile game developed by PikselOL Games.

1. Information we collect

PikselOL Games does not collect personal information such as your name, e-mail address, phone number or location, and does not send such information to its servers. The Game requires no account or sign-in; the optional Google Play sign-in is used only for cloud backup (section 2).

Your game progress (gems, store items, unit upgrades, missions, zombie records, number of wins, saved match) and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Game; if you have a cloud backup, you can restore it with your Google Play account or your recovery code (section 2).

2. Online features

The Game's online features run on Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer/Relay, Leaderboards, Cloud Save, Analytics). They all use the same anonymous player ID; we do not ask for your name or e-mail. In Classic and Zombie modes the opponent is controlled by your device.

  • Online Battle (starts only when you open this mode): During a match, your game commands (training units, building towers, advancing ages, using powers), the match state and the look you chose in the store (colors and decorations) are sent to your opponent. There is no chat in the Game. Your IP address is processed by Unity's servers to make the connection; a room code is valid only for that match.
  • World ranking (Unity Leaderboards): When you win a Classic match, your total number of Classic wins is sent to the ranking, linked to your anonymous player ID and an automatically generated player name. That name and your number of wins are visible to other players in the ranking. When you open the ranking with the Ranking button in the main menu, the top 10 players and your own rank are read from Unity.
  • Cloud backup (Unity Cloud Save): If you signed in with Google Play or got a recovery code under Settings → Backup & Account, your game progress (gems, store items, unit upgrades, mission progress and claimed rewards, numbers of finished matches and wins, zombie records) is saved on Unity's servers, linked to your anonymous player ID, and updated automatically after every match and when the Game goes to the background. Your settings and an unfinished match are not backed up. The recovery code is linked to your anonymous account as a username and password: anyone who knows the code can access the backup, so do not share it. Entering the code on a new phone loads the backup onto that device.
  • Google Play sign-in (Google Play Games Services, Unity Authentication): So that your backup comes back on a new phone, the Game offers to sign in with your Google Play Games account. The sign-in window opens once, the first time you go to choose a match; if you decline, the Game continues normally. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game may connect automatically at start. When you sign in, Google gives the Game a one-time authorization code; Unity uses it to verify your Google Play Games player ID and links your cloud backup to it. If a backup made on another phone with the same Google account is found, you are asked whether to load it. Your e-mail address and Google password are not given to the Game. Google Play Games is governed by Google's privacy policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and settings in the Play Games app.
  • Usage statistics (Unity Analytics): To improve the Game, when a match starts and ends the game mode, difficulty, whether the match was won, the age reached and the match duration, together with device information (model, operating system, language, country), are sent to Unity, linked to your anonymous player ID. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, usage statistics are collected only if you allow it in the consent form.
  • Notifications: If you allow them (permission is asked once, after you finish your first match), reminders such as "your mission reward is ready" or "your army is waiting" are scheduled on your device itself; no data is sent for this. You can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Update check: When the Game starts and when it comes back to the foreground, it downloads a small version file from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages, pikselol36700.github.io) to see whether the installed version is still supported. No personal information is sent with this request; as with any web request, your IP address is seen by GitHub (GitHub privacy statement). If your version is too old, the Game directs you to update it on Google Play.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The Game is free and shows ads (rewarded ads and a banner in the main menu). There are no in-app purchases in the Game. Ads are served by the Unity LevelPlay mediation service and its partner ad networks (e.g. Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob). To show ads, cap ad frequency, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, these services may automatically collect:

  • The advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, view time) and app usage information

This information is governed by the providers' own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Unity Gaming Services): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google (AdMob, Google Play Services and Google Play Games): policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Play links in the Game open those apps or sites, where their own terms apply.

4. Opting out of personalized ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before showing ads; usage statistics are collected or not according to the same choice. You can change your choice at any time with the Settings → Privacy options button in the Game.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads. You will then see non-personalized ads.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been collected, please contact us at the address below.

6. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device and we have no access to it. Your anonymous player ID, your ranking name and number of wins, (if you signed in with Google Play) its link to your Google Play Games player ID, (if you got a recovery code) the username and password tied to the code, your cloud backup (if any) and usage statistics are stored on Unity Gaming Services; you can delete them from inside the Game or ask us by e-mail to delete them, as described in section 7. Data collected by ad providers is stored for the periods and with the safeguards described in their policies.

7. Deleting your Stickman Ages data

You can delete your PikselOL Games account and data for Stickman Ages in these ways:

  1. Inside the Game: In the main menu, tap Settings → Backup & Account → Delete Account and confirm with Delete (an internet connection is required). Your online player account, Google Play Games link, recovery code and cloud backup are deleted immediately and permanently, and Unity is asked to delete your usage statistics. This cannot be undone.
  2. By e-mail (if you can no longer install the Game, or if you also want your ranking name and score removed): send an e-mail with the subject "Stickman Ages data deletion" to pikselol36700@gmail.com. So that we can find your account, include one of these: your recovery code, the player name shown in the ranking, or your Google Play Games player name. Requests by e-mail are completed within 30 days at the latest, and we confirm them by e-mail.
  3. Progress on your device (gems, store items, upgrades, missions, saved match, settings): deleted by uninstalling the Game or with "Clear data" for Stickman Ages in Android settings. "Delete Account" does not delete the progress on this phone; you can keep playing.

What is deleted: your anonymous player account, Google Play Games link and recovery code, and your cloud backup. If you ask by e-mail, your ranking name and score are deleted as well.

What is not deleted: Your ranking score cannot be deleted from inside the Game; after your account is deleted it stays under the automatically assigned nickname and is no longer linked to any account. You can ask for its removal by e-mail as described above. For data collected by ad networks, you can delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads. You can delete the Play Games profile that Google keeps in the Play Games app; that does not delete our data.
